# Business setup, clients and catalogue (v0)

Normative for the setup features of both apps: onboarding, the business profile, clients, the item catalogue,
numbering-series settings, custom tax rates (GENERIC) and images. iOS implements it in `InvoiceCore` (rules and
drafts), `InvoiceData` (storage) and `InvoiceUI` (screens); Android in `:core:domain`, `:core:data` and `:app`.
Field shapes are in `schema/domain.schema.json`; tax rules are in `tax/ENGINE.md`. Field validation rules are proven
by `fixtures/validation/fields.json` (kind `field`).

## 1. Records

- **Ids** are new lowercase UUIDs generated by the app. **Timestamps** are epoch ms UTC: `created_at` is set once,
  `updated_at` on every write.
- **Text** is trimmed on save; an optional field that is empty after trimming is stored as `NULL`.
- **Delete** writes a tombstone (`deleted_at = now`); it never issues SQL `DELETE` on synced tables, so the deletion
  syncs as an ordinary update. Tombstoned rows are invisible everywhere (lists, pickers, lookups, duplicate checks).
- **Archive** (clients and catalogue items) sets `archived_at`. Archived rows are hidden from pickers and from the
  default list, shown under "Archived", and can be restored. Documents keep their own snapshots, so archiving or
  deleting a client or item never changes an existing document.

## 2. Device and active business

- On first launch the app creates this device's `device_state` row: `id` = a new UUID (the `owner_device_id` of the
  numbering series it creates), `device_name` = the platform device name, `preferences = {}`. The row is local: never
  synced and never backed up. Only one row exists; if several are found, the earliest `created_at` wins.
- `preferences` is a JSON object; v0 keys: `activeBusinessId` (string).
- **Active business:** `preferences.activeBusinessId` when it names a live business; otherwise the live business with
  the earliest `created_at` (ties: lowest `id`); none → onboarding. v1 shows no business picker unless there are
  two or more live businesses (ADR-0015).

## 3. Onboarding

Five steps; the business is created only by **Finish**, in one transaction with its numbering series and the
`activeBusinessId` preference. Nothing is written before Finish; closing the app mid-way restarts onboarding.

| Step | Fields | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Country | `countryCode` (from `reference/countries.json`), `homeCurrency` | `IN` → tax config `IN`, `GB` → `GB`, any other country → `GENERIC`. IN/GB use the config's `currency`; GENERIC asks for the home currency (`reference/currencies.json`, required). |
| 2 Registration | `taxRegistration` (the config's `registrations`, default the first); IN turnover tier; GENERIC first tax rate | IN regular/composition: "Turnover in the previous financial year" with one choice per `productCodes.tiers` entry (§3.1). GENERIC with a tax-charging registration: tax name (default `Tax`) and rate percent (required) → custom rates (§7). |
| 3 Business | `name` (required), `legalName`, `taxId`, address (`line1` required; `line2`, `city`, `postalCode`, `regionCode`), `email`, `phone`, `website`, IN `extraIds.pan`, GB `extraIds.companyNumber` + `extraIds.registeredOffice` | Tax ID: shown when the config has a `taxIdFormats` entry and the registration `requiresTaxId` (then required), or as an optional free-text "Tax ID" for GENERIC charging registrations. Validated live with `ENGINE.md` §8; invalid values block Finish. IN: `regionCode` is required; when a GSTIN is valid, `regionCode` = its region (read-only) and an empty PAN is prefilled with GSTIN characters 3–12. Field rules: §8. |
| 4 Bank & UPI | `bank.accountName`, `accountNumber`, `bankName`, IN `ifsc`, GB `sortCode`, `iban`, `swift`; IN `upiVpa` | All optional; rules in §8. |
| 5 Logo & signature | logo image, signature (drawn) | Optional; processed per §9. |

Finish creates the business with the defaults `paymentTermsDays = 15`, `templateId = "modern"`,
`accentColor = null` (templates then use the first `design/tokens.json` accent preset), `reminderDaysAfterDue = null`,
`turnoverMinor` per §3.1, `customRates` per §7 (GENERIC only, otherwise `null`), and one numbering series per
document type (§6).

### 3.1 Turnover tier (India)
The app asks for the tier, never the exact turnover. The first tier stores `turnoverMinor = null` (the engine then
uses the first tier); tier *k* > 1 stores the previous tier's `maxTurnoverMinor + 1`. Labels come from the tier
bounds: "Up to ₹5 crore" / "More than ₹5 crore" for `IN.json` v2025-09-22.

## 4. Business profile (Settings)

Every onboarding field stays editable, plus: `paymentTermsDays` (0–365), `defaultNotes`, `defaultTerms`,
`templateId` (`classic`, `modern`, `minimal`, `compact`), `accentColor` (one of the token accent presets),
`reminderDaysAfterDue` (0–60 or none), and, for IN regular dealers, `extraIds.lutReference` + `lutValidUntil`
(exports under LUT; required by the `lut_reference_missing` check). `countryCode`, `taxConfig` and `homeCurrency`
are fixed after onboarding in v1. Changing the registration or tax ID affects documents issued afterwards only
(issued documents keep their seller snapshot).

## 5. Clients

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| `name` | Required. |
| `isBusiness` | B2B when true (default false). |
| `countryCode` | Required; default the business country. A client is **domestic** when `countryCode` equals the business country. |
| `regionCode` | Offered for domestic clients when the config has `regions` (active regions only, sorted by name). Optional; when a domestic B2B client has a valid GSTIN, `regionCode` = its region (read-only). Cleared for foreign clients. |
| `taxId` | Shown when `isBusiness`. Domestic: validated with the config's tax ID format (`ENGINE.md` §8) and stored normalised; an invalid value blocks saving. Foreign: free text, trimmed and upper-cased. Optional in both cases. Cleared when `isBusiness` is false. |
| `billingAddress`, `shippingAddress` | Optional; `line1` is required once any other address field is filled. `countryCode` of an address = the client's `countryCode`; `regionCode` = the client's `regionCode`. Postal code rules (§8) apply to IN and GB addresses. |
| `email` | Optional; §8 `email`. |
| `contactName`, `phone`, `notes` | Optional free text. |
| `defaultCurrency` | Optional; `NULL` = the business home currency. |

- **Duplicate tax ID:** saving a client whose normalised `taxId` equals another live client's shows a warning, not
  an error (there are no UNIQUE constraints; ADR-0015).
- **Search** (list and pickers): case-insensitive substring match of the query (trimmed) against `name`,
  `contactName`, `email`, `phone` and `taxId`; results sorted by name (locale-aware, case-insensitive), then `id`.

## 6. Numbering series

- Finish (§3) creates one series per document type: `docType` `invoice` / `quote`, `label` "Invoices" / "Quotes",
  `pattern` = the config's `numbering.invoicePattern` / `quotePattern`, `reset` = `numbering.reset`,
  `ownerDeviceId` = this device, `counters = {}`.
- Only the owner device edits a series (`ownerDeviceId` = this device); others see it read-only (ADR-0015).
- Editable: `label`, `pattern`, `reset`, and the **next number** of the current period (for users continuing from
  another tool). A pattern must contain exactly one `{seq:N}` token (N = 1–9); `{`…`}` groups that are not
  `ENGINE.md` §5 tokens are rejected. The preview is the number for today with the next sequence, and a pattern whose
  preview fails the §5 result checks (`number_too_long`, `number_invalid_chars`) cannot be saved.
- The next number is `counters[periodKey(today)] ?? 1`; setting it writes that counter. It must be ≥ 1 and, from Phase
  2a on, greater than the highest sequence already issued in that period.

## 7. Custom tax rates (GENERIC)

- Stored in `business.customRates` using the tax-config `rate` shape with `components`.
- Onboarding with a tax-charging registration creates, from the tax name *T* and percent *p*: `{ id: <new id>,
  category: "standard", label: "T p%", effectiveFrom: <today>, components: [{ code: CODE(T), label: T, percent: p }] }`
  then the "No tax" rate. A registration that does not charge tax creates only the "No tax" rate, so every item has a
  valid rate if the business registers later.
- **"No tax" rate:** `{ id: "none", category: "zero", label: "No tax", effectiveFrom: "2000-01-01",
  components: [{ code: "TAX", label: "Tax", percent: "0" }] }`.
- New rate ids are `r` + the first 8 hex digits of a new UUID; if a rate of the business already has that id,
  `r` + all 32 hex digits. `CODE(T)` = `T` upper-cased with every character outside `A–Z 0–9` removed, at most 12
  characters, `TAX` if empty.
- Settings lists, adds and edits custom rates (label, one component, or two with the second optionally `compound`).
  A rate used by a live catalogue item cannot be removed.

## 8. Field rules

Each rule normalises the input, then validates it. The same rule is used live (inline message) and on save.
`fixtures/validation/fields.json` is the answer key; error codes are `format` and `checksum`.

| Rule | Normalise | Valid when |
|---|---|---|
| `email` | trim | `^[^\s@]+@[^\s@]+\.[^\s@]+$` |
| `ifsc` | remove whitespace, upper-case | `^[A-Z]{4}0[A-Z0-9]{6}$` |
| `upiVpa` | trim, lower-case | `^[a-z0-9._-]{2,256}@[a-z][a-z0-9.-]{1,63}$` |
| `sortCode` | remove whitespace and `-`; valid values are output as `NN-NN-NN` | 6 digits |
| `iban` | remove whitespace, upper-case | `^[A-Z]{2}[0-9]{2}[A-Z0-9]{11,30}$`, then ISO 7064 mod 97: move the first 4 characters to the end, replace letters with 10–35, the number mod 97 must be 1 (`checksum`) |
| `bic` | remove whitespace, upper-case | `^[A-Z]{6}[A-Z0-9]{2}([A-Z0-9]{3})?$` |
| `pan` | remove whitespace, upper-case | `^[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]$` |
| `postalCodeIN` | remove whitespace | `^[1-9][0-9]{5}$` |
| `postalCodeGB` | upper-case, remove whitespace, then insert one space before the last 3 characters (if longer than 3) | `^[A-Z]{1,2}[0-9][A-Z0-9]? [0-9][A-Z]{2}$` |
| `companyNumberGB` | remove whitespace, upper-case; 1–7 digits are left-padded with `0` to 8 | `^([0-9]{8}|[A-Z]{2}[0-9]{6})$` |
| `hsnSac` | remove whitespace | `^[0-9]{2,8}$` |

`normalized` is reported for valid and invalid values alike. Tax IDs use `ENGINE.md` §8, not this table.

## 9. Images (logo, signature)

- **Logo:** decode, apply the EXIF orientation, downscale so the longest side is at most 1024 px (never upscale).
  Encode as PNG when the image has any transparent pixel, otherwise JPEG at quality 0.85. If the result exceeds
  300 KB: JPEG retries at quality 0.7, 0.55 and 0.4; then (PNG or JPEG) the image is scaled by 0.75 and the same
  encoding sequence runs again, until it fits. Re-encoding drops all metadata (EXIF, GPS).
- **Signature:** drawn with a finger or pencil in dark ink on a transparent canvas; exported as PNG cropped to the ink
  bounds plus 8 px of padding, longest side at most 1024 px. An empty drawing cannot be saved.
- Stored as an `asset` row: `kind`, `mime` (`image/png` or `image/jpeg`), `sha256` = lowercase hex SHA-256 of the
  stored bytes, `data`. `business.logo_asset_id` / `signature_asset_id` point at it.
- **Reuse:** storing bytes whose `sha256` matches a live asset of the same business and kind returns that asset.
- **Replace/remove:** the business points at the new asset (or `NULL`); the old asset is tombstoned when no live
  business references it.

## 10. Catalogue items

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|

| `kind` | `goods` or `service` (default `service`). |
| `unit` | A `reference/units.json` id; default `NOS` for goods, `OTH` for services. |

**Search:** case-insensitive substring match against `name`, `description` and `productCode`; sorted by name, then `id`.

## 11. Typed amounts and decimals

Both parsers are proven by `fixtures/format/input.json` (kind `input`).

- **Money:** remove `,` and all whitespace; empty → `empty`. The text must then match `^[0-9]+(\.[0-9]*)?$` or
  `^\.[0-9]+$` (`invalid`), with at most the currency's `minorUnits` fraction digits (`tooManyDecimals`). Minor units =
  the exact decimal × 10^exp; above 10^15 → `tooLarge`. Negative amounts are not accepted in v0.
- **Decimals** (quantities, percents): trim; empty → `empty`; the text must match `^[0-9]+(\.[0-9]*)?$` or
  `^\.[0-9]+$` (`invalid`). The result is the canonical decimal string (`ENGINE.md` §7.2 plus: leading zeros removed,
  `.5` → `0.5`).
