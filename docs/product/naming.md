# App name (Phase 0 task 7)

"InvoiceBuilder" is a working name. Generic names are crowded on both stores (App Store search, 2026-09-19:
"Invoice Maker – Get Paid Fast", "Invoice2go", "Invoice Simple", "Invoice Home", "Freebie Invoice Maker", …), and an
App Store name must be unique, so pick a **short, distinctive brand** and put the keywords in the subtitle.

- **Title:** brand only, ≤ 30 characters (App Store) / ≤ 30 (Play).
- **App Store subtitle / Play short description:** "GST & VAT invoices on your phone" (keywords: invoice, GST, VAT,
  estimate, quote, bill).
- **Checks before committing:** reserve the name by creating the App Store Connect app record (the definitive
  availability check), search Play Console, the UK IPO and Indian trademark registries, and `.app`/`.in` domains.
- Avoid names close to established Indian accounting brands (Tally, Vyapar, Zoho, Khatabook, myBillBook).

The bundle / application ID can be chosen independently of the display name (e.g. `app.<brand>.invoices`) and is
needed before Phase 1 (StoreKit product IDs derive from it).
