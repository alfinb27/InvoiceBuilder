# Product brief (v1)

**Who:** sole traders, freelancers and small shops in India and the UK who need professional, compliant invoices from
their phone (and, for shops, from a counter iPad or Android tablet).

**Promise:** create and send a compliant invoice in under a minute; your data never leaves your device (except your
own iCloud, if you turn sync on).

**v1 scope:** business profile, clients, item catalogue, invoices and quotes with India GST (incl. exports under LUT,
reverse charge, composition) and UK VAT (incl. domestic reverse charge, services outside the scope), tax-inclusive
prices, 4 PDF templates with UPI QR and signature, sharing (WhatsApp, email, print, files), payments and derived status,
local overdue reminders, portable backup/restore, iCloud sync (Apple devices), iPad and Android tablet layouts,
15 free invoices then a one-time unlock.

**Not in v1:** e-invoicing (IRN/QR), payment processing, accounting, user accounts, a backend, Hindi UI, credit notes,
recurring invoices, reports, CSV export, multi-business, cross-platform sync (see `docs/plan.md` Phase 8).

**Pricing (to decide before Phase 5):** one non-consumable. The plan suggests testing £1.99–2.99 / ₹99–149 instead of
£0.99 (≈ £0.70 net after VAT and 15% commission). Price is store configuration and can change after launch.
