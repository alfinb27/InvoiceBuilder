**Last updated:** <date>

<App name> is made by <developer name>. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

**Your invoices stay with you.** Business details, clients, items, invoices, quotes, payments, logos and signatures are
stored on your device. We do not run servers that receive this data, and we cannot see it.

**iCloud sync (Apple devices, optional).** If you turn on sync, your data is stored in your own private iCloud
database, protected by your Apple Account, so it appears on your other Apple devices. Apple processes it under
Apple's privacy policy. We cannot access it.

**Backups.** Backup files are created only when you export them, and they are saved or shared wherever you choose.
Your device's own backups (iCloud device backup, Android Auto Backup to your Google Drive) may include the app's data
according to your device settings.

**Purchases.** Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We receive only confirmation that the unlock was purchased,
not your payment details.

**Analytics and tracking.** The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking. Apple and Google may
share anonymous crash reports with us if you have opted in to sharing diagnostics with developers.

**Sharing.** When you share an invoice (for example by WhatsApp or email), the PDF goes to the app and recipient you
choose.

**Children.** The app is intended for businesses and is not directed at children.

**Your choices.** You can delete your data at any time by deleting it in the app or deleting the app (and, if you use
sync, the app's data in iCloud settings).

**Contact.** <contact email>
